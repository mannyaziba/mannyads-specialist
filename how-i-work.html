Method

How I work, and how to judge it

I do not show case stories or results here. Any case I describe publicly will have a source record and written client permission first. Until then, judge the work by the method and by a free triage on your own case.

The six-step framework

  1. Identify. Read the exact notice and establish the policy cited.
  2. Investigate. Check the site, ads, payments and recent account changes against that policy.
  3. Fix first. Make the changes Google flagged before any appeal is sent.
  4. Write the appeal. A factual, policy-grounded account of the cause and the fixes.
  5. Submit and track. File through the proper channel and log every step.
  6. Follow up. Respond to Google's replies and decide on further rounds.

What you get to check

  • A written diagnosis tied to the wording of your notice.
  • A fix list you can verify on your own site.
  • The appeal text before it is submitted.
  • A clear statement of what is uncertain.
Limits
Some cases are not recoverable. If I think yours is not, the triage will say so.
